Second full read of `Frontend/` against the project rubric
([`docs/ux.md`](../ux.md), [`docs/accessibility.md`](../accessibility.md)), taken
as the opening of the refinement cycle in
[`docs/briefs/ux-realignment/plan.md`](../briefs/ux-realignment/plan.md). It is

and its [remediation](ux-remediation-2026-11-10.md): same five dimensions, same
weights, same severity rule, same score formula. No code was changed by the
audit itself; the only writes are this file and its screenshots.

## 2. Scores

`dimension = 10 − (0.2·n1 + 0.5·n2 + 1.5·n3 + 3·n4)`, floor 0, capped at 5 when
the dimension holds a severity-4 finding. Same five dimensions and weights as
the baseline.

| States and copy | 15 % | 2 | 4 | 0 | 0 | −2.4 | 7.6 | 1.14 |

| Platform and perceived responsiveness | 15 % | 1 | 4 | 0 | 0 | −2.2 | 7.8 | 1.17 |
| **Total** | 100 % | **11** | **25** | **2** | **0** | | | **6.8** |

**Score: 6.8 / 10.** 38 findings (11 severity 1, 25 severity 2, 2 severity 3,
0 severity 4), from 43 findings at 3.6 on 2026-11-10 and the 9.9 that the
remediation recorded the same day.

Read the number for what it is, and read the direction it moved in, not just the
delta. The formula is linear in the *count* of findings, so it measures how many
defects a pass of a given breadth found, not how bad the app is. Two facts have
to sit next to the 6.8:

1. **This is the first pass that looked at the screens.** The 9.9 of
   2026-11-10 is described by its own document as "a code-evidence
   re-measurement, not a second live audit", and it did not open the app. Every
   one of the 17 rows in "design system and visual coherence" (§3.4) is
   composition, hierarchy or rhythm visible only in a rendered screen — 23 at
   1280, `04` at 390, `34` with the panel open. A code read cannot produce them,
   which is why the baseline scored that dimension 8.1 and the remediation 10.0.
   The 1.4 is therefore not "the design system got worse"; it is "the design
   system is now measured on what it draws".
2. **The other four dimensions moved from ~10.0 to 7.6-7.9 for a sharper
   reason.** The deeper checks found what the earlier passes did not ask for:
   the constructed link between a creation effect and a cancel (F44), the
   destroyed draft owner (F58), the aria reading of a button-shaped card (F50),
   the literal `&apos;` (F60). Those are new findings, not regressions.

**Two readings of the same table**, stated so nobody quotes one of them out of
context:

| Reading | What it counts | Number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everything this pass found | 38 rows, formula as it stands | 6.8 / 10 |
| What blocks a user | the two severity-3 rows (F44, F91) | both are one-file fixes, both are in §4 |

No dimension holds a severity-4 finding, so the cap does not apply. The two
severity-3 rows are the only ones that lose information or operability rather
than polish it.

### 3.4 Design system and visual coherence

This is the dimension the 2026-11-10 passes could not measure. It is read off
the captures first and the source second: every row names the PNG that shows the
defect and the `file:line` that causes it.

**The reading, screen by screen** (what the eye hits first, what competes, where
the void is):

- **Login** (`01`, `35`). A centred stack anchored 24 px below the safe area, not
a centred screen: the form's top edge sits at y≈345 and the last 300 px of 844
hold air. Three elements claim "first" — a 64 px accent disc, a 28 px wordmark,
a full-width primary — and the primary is the least specific of the three
because the accent is already spent twice above it.
- **Register** (`10`). The best rhythm in the app, because the card's internal
12 px beat is visibly tighter than the 16 px between blocks. It shares no
masthead, no width and no alignment with login: two first impressions of one
product.
- **Catalog** (`02`, `20`, `23`, `27`, `34`, `32`). The only screen whose reading
order is unambiguous at 390: title → filter card → section line → first card.
Inside each card three things compete — an uppercase pill, a two-line 19 px
sentence and a wrapping 13 px meta run — and the pill wins while repeating the
same word down the whole list. At 1280 the frame falls apart: title and cards
start at x=145, the filter card ends at x≈1116, the service cards at x≈855, the
scrollbar at x≈1125 — three right edges, 260 px of unused column beside every
card, and the 48 px nav disc isolated 1000 px from the title it belongs to. With
the filter panel open (`34`) the first result starts at y≈790, below the fold.
- **Match** (`03`, `24`). The emptiest screen and the least defensible: a 28 px
title, a 17 px "Non ancora disponibile", two lines of 13 px grey, then 700 px of
nothing at 1280. No frame, no icon, no action, no route out. Three type sizes
inside 20 px of each other, so nothing leads.
- **Offer and request** (`04`, `21`, `13`). A 190 px opening block of prose and a
section title before the first item, and inside a card two uppercase pills that
repeat the section heading, a 15 px paragraph with no title rank, then three
50 px buttons of the same weight where "Elimina" wraps onto its own row at both
390 and 360.
- **Agreements** (`05`, `14`, `25`). The strongest single component in the app:
the three-option pill with count badges. Below it, one section header with a
leading icon, an outlined ring empty state — then 330 px of void at 390 and
470 px at 1280, with no action on the screen. Two accent discs sit at opposite
corners doing two unrelated jobs (`05`).
- **Profile** (`06`, `15`, `26`). An 84 px accent disc with 30 px initials — the
largest type in the app — then five peer cards of identical weight, of which one
is a single sentence about session validity. Titles do not share a left edge
(x=40 vs x=62) and use two ranks for one role (`heading` 19 on two cards,
`subheading` 17 on the two beside them). At 1280 the column is 524 px centred:
a third frame in a four-screen set.
- **Edit profile** (`09`). A back arrow and a title on one row — the only title
row in the app with a leading control, copy-pasted in three screens — then an
untitled card of fields where register's equivalent is titled. Border inside
border (card 1 px + input 1 px) makes the densest screen also the busiest, and
the "Valore attuale" hint in the smallest grey reads as an error.
- **Staff reports** (`18`, `26`, `29`). Title row, a ghost button used as a
cross-screen link, a four-line instruction paragraph (the loudest block above
the fold), five outlined chips wrapping 4+1 with the fifth orphaned, then the
empty state with a filled "Ricarica". Two different empty patterns on one
screen.
- **Staff users** (`19`, `30`, `22`, `31`). The same title row and the same
instruction paragraph, a filter card holding a 50 px search field and two chip
rows. At 360 the pair of row actions breaks: "Rendi staff" wraps to two lines
beside a one-line "Sospendi", so the two buttons in one row have different
heights. No count on the list anywhere.
- **The modal shell** (`07`, `17`). The best-composed panel in the app: title +
44 pt close, pill, headline, labelled meta block, grey well, filled primary. Its
last line — a full sentence set in 11 px uppercase with 1.2 tracking — is the
loudest object in the lower half and the least readable, and it sits at the
moment of highest intent.

**Rules that exist and hold**: one 16 px mobile frame on every capture in both
themes; one surface recipe (`Card.jsx:5-27`); one modal shell for six panels; one
type scale with no ad-hoc `fontSize` in the screens (F37 held); one device for
"active choice" (the filled accent pill); dark parity with no inverted surface
and no shadow appearing where light has none.

**Rules that exist and are broken**: one page frame (the catalog is a 1024 frame
whose cards stop at 712, every other screen is 560 centred, the auth pair is a
third composition); one section-to-section gap (a section is only 4 px further
from its neighbour than two rows of one card); one card anatomy (four species,
the same field set at `heading` in one and `body` in another); the same rank for
the same content; card titles on one left edge; one error anatomy (catalog draws
banner *then* a separate retry); an empty state that carries the way out
(agreements does not); one shape for a segmented choice (three shapes for three
segmented choices); a list that states its size (both staff lists say nothing);
one label device (`caption` is the pill, the chip, the list count header and
full printed sentences at once).

**Rules missing outright**: a page-header component (eleven screens compose the
title block by hand, so four have a subtitle, three have an instruction
paragraph, one has nothing); a job for the subtitle (six of seven describe how to
operate the interface instead of what the screen is — `app-screens.md` §4.3);
where a sub-screen's title row goes (`BackButton` + title copy-pasted three
times, `report.jsx` uses a bottom button instead); what an unavailable tab should
look like (`EmptyState`'s variant leaves 88 % of the viewport empty); how the
accent is allocated (four discs of four sizes, every ghost label, the active nav
row and every category pill, so nothing is left to mark the action); a max
height or scroll region for a panel.

## 6. Method notes worth keeping

- The app's theme preference is persisted (`barattolo.tema`), so the browser
  theme must be set through storage, not only through `emulateMedia`. Half of
  the first capture pass was lost to this: the files looked right and were dark.
- The PHP dev server in the container is single-threaded: a screen that fires
  three requests takes seconds, and a fixed `waitForTimeout` captures a spinner.
  Captures wait for the absence of the app's loading words plus a stable number
  of interactive nodes, which is what the 2026-11-10 pass did by hand.
- `tsc --noEmit` in this repo gates syntax and import resolution only, exactly
  as `docs/decisions.md` D010 states (`allowJs` without `checkJs`, no
  `@ts-check` anywhere): measured clean in 5.2 s, and it did not catch the
  literal `&apos;` of F60. It is kept for what it does, not credited with more.

## 7. Not verified — and what it would take

1. **The agreements bell panel.** The demo accounts have no pending proposal, so
   the bell has nothing to open. Needs a seeded incoming proposal.
2. **Any rendered accessibility tree.** F50, F51, F54, F56 need one
   `accessibility.snapshot()` per screen; the static claim is what the code
   does, not what the platform announces.
3. **200 % text on the six panels (F70).** Needs the halved-viewport recipe
   applied per panel, not only to the catalog.
4. **Narrow-width wrapping (F71).** Decided by computed metrics, not source.
5. **The login transition order (F58).** Needs a 401, a re-login and a
   `location.pathname` read; the row is a code-level conflict, the reproduction
   would only decide how often it loses.
6. **The operations log's values (F61).** The backend payload for
   `action`/`target.type` was not read.
7. **F94's line of code.** The react-native-web overlay is VERIFIED in `32`, but
the source line that renders a bare string in the catalog's failure path needs
the browser console stack, which this session's capture did not collect. Until
then the row carries no `file:line`.
8. **Screen-reader behaviour on iOS** (F53's live regions are Android-only props
   in React Native). Unchanged from the baseline.
